Version: 0.1.3
Date: 2026-11-12
  Balancing:
    - The radar only charts while someone is driving or riding in the buggy. A parked buggy is no longer a free radar.
    - The built-in alternator only powers the equipment grid while the buggy is occupied. Parked, its equipment runs on what the batteries hold.
    - Top speed is 80% of the car's, on every ground and fuel. The engine uses about two thirds of the car's power, so fuel lasts longer too.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - Custom sounds: engine start, running loop and shutdown, hatch open and close, and brake screech.
    - Radar ping plays to the driver and passenger as the buggy charts. Per-player setting: seconds between pings, 0 = off.
  Changes:
    - Wheel dust is back, coming from the buggy's own rear wheels instead of under the body. Each ground type keeps its own dust colour.
    - Ground with no dust of its own (paved floors, modded tiles without their own dust) now gets light tan dust.
    - Removed the exhaust smoke. It came from the middle of the body and drew over the sphere.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-07
  Features:
    - Observation buggy: a spherical scout car with 64-direction sprites.
    - Charts the map around itself like a radar on wheels (radius and interval in mod settings).
    - Unlocked with Automobilism.
    - Paint takes on the player colour, like vanilla vehicles.
    - 4x4 equipment grid, powered by a hidden built-in 1 MW alternator (all 16 cells free).
